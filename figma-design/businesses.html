Key Business Verticals

A vertically integrated group — manufacturing, retail, customization and fit-out — operating under one roof so quality is owned at every stage.

Manufacturing & Production

Four in-house production houses, each built around a single craft.

Maylaa Chocolate Factory

Our in-house chocolate and confectionery unit, known for handcrafted elegance and gourmet-grade creations that combine tradition with luxury. Trading as Daima.

First Perfumes

A state-of-the-art fragrance manufacturing facility delivering bespoke and large-scale perfume production — home to Saleel, Juvenis, Blue Ocean, Jouri, Fabian, Lencia and Asrar.

Maylaa Gold & Diamonds

A luxury jewellery division specializing in bespoke, bulk and B2B solutions, renowned for fine craftsmanship, timeless design and regional relevance.

SpaceConcept

An interior fit-out and retail architecture brand that transforms spaces through custom-built solutions — merging form, function and brand identity.

Our Production Philosophy

Production is more than a process — it is a promise of quality, innovation and authenticity. Whether we are creating luxurious perfumes, gourmet chocolates or bespoke jewellery, our ethos is rooted in a deep respect for craftsmanship and a relentless pursuit of perfection.

Every product is a reflection of our dedication to detail, precision and heritage.

Tradition

Manual Craftsmanship

Each Daima chocolate is handcrafted to preserve texture and aesthetic. Our perfumers create blends using age-old layering techniques, and jewellers at Maylaa Gold & Diamonds manually set stones and engrave pieces — ensuring uniqueness in every design.

Technology

Automated Excellence

Advanced filling and bottling systems for perfumes, temperature-controlled molding for chocolates, and high-precision CNC machinery for jewellery. This fusion of hands-on care and tech-driven consistency lets us scale without losing quality.
