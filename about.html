Our Story

Established in 1999, Maylaa International Trading LLC began as a vision to bridge lifestyle segments across the UAE. What started as a single store has today flourished into a multi-vertical organization with a growing portfolio of luxury businesses.

From perfumes and fashion accessories to gourmet chocolates, bespoke jewellery and premium floral arrangements — Maylaa International has built a legacy grounded in trust, style and excellence.

Maylaa is a name that stands for trust, taste and timeless style — curating experiences for customers who appreciate quality and cultural relevance.

Our Purpose

Every brand tells a story — and every product is a promise. At Maylaa, we are committed to creating meaningful experiences, delivering exceptional quality, and enhancing lives through every item we offer.

Our Mission

To deliver excellence through innovation, authenticity and value. We aim to become a destination point for luxury fashion, building long-term relationships with our customers, partners, suppliers and communities while continually setting new benchmarks in the industries we serve.
